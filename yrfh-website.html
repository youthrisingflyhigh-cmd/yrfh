Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 26, 2026 · Youth Rising Fly High · EIN: 86-1229572

1. Overview

Youth Rising Fly High ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. We serve youth and families and take the protection of personal information — especially that of minors — seriously.

2. Information we collect

  • Name, age, and contact information provided when registering for programs.
  • Parent or guardian name and contact information for minors.
  • Donation information (name, amount, contact) for tax receipt purposes.
  • Basic website usage data (page visits, browser type) via standard analytics.

3. How we use your information

  • To register and communicate with program participants and families.
  • To send program schedules, updates, and community announcements.
  • To process and acknowledge donations and provide tax receipts.
  • To improve our programs and website experience.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

4. Children's privacy

We are committed to protecting the privacy of minors. We collect information about minors only with verifiable parental or guardian consent, in compliance with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act).

5. Photos and media

Photos or videos taken at our events may be used for nonprofit promotional purposes only with a signed release form from the participant's parent or guardian. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us.

6. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorized access or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Your rights

You may request to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you or your child at any time by contacting us at youthrisinflyhigh@gmail.com.

8. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will post changes on this page with a revised effective date.
